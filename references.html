Try It Yourself

You can see which HTTP version a website uses right in your browser:

  1. Open any website in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox and press F12.
  2. Click the Network tab and reload the page.
  3. Right-click the column headers and turn on the Protocol column.
  4. Look for h2 (HTTP/2), h3 (HTTP/3), or http/1.1.
